# Apple-Inspired Design System

Reference source: `VoltAgent/awesome-design-md`, `design-md/apple/DESIGN.md` (MIT).

## Product Direction

Pressure Gauge Agent uses an Apple-inspired operational interface: the content is the focus, chrome is quiet, actions are clear, and visual density is intentionally low. This is a design reference only; do not use Apple trademarks, logos, marketing copy, or product imagery.

## Tokens

- Action color: `#0066cc`; use it for actionable links, focus, and primary buttons only.
- Surfaces: white `#ffffff`, parchment `#f5f5f7`, and dark focus surface `#272729`.
- Text: primary `#1d1d1f`, secondary `#6e6e73`.
- Typography: `SF Pro Display` for headings, `SF Pro Text` for UI/body, with system fallbacks.
- Radius: `8px` compact controls, `11px` inputs, `18px` utility cards, `999px` action pills.

## Rules

- Use generous whitespace and clear hierarchy before adding borders or shadows.
- Do not use decorative gradients, oversized shadows, or competing accent colors.
- Body text is 17px on desktop when space permits; headings use tight negative tracking.
- Primary actions are blue pills. Secondary actions are blue text or outlined pills.
- Minimum touch target is 44px on mobile.
- Preserve content readability and operational efficiency on data-heavy pages.
